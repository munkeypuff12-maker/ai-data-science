Dataset of a survey conducted in 2019 assessing climbing injuries. Includes respondent's years of experience, sex, grade of climbing, and specific injury
  Columns:
    Respondent ID: Numeric id of the respondent in the survey
    Collector ID: Numeric id of the conductor of the survey
    Start Date: Time (h/min/sec) of the start of the survey
    End date: Time (h/min/sec) of the end of the survey
    IP Address: IP Address of the respondent
    First Name: First name of respondent (Left blank in respect for privacy)
    Last Name: Last name of respondent (Left blank in respect for privacy)
    What is your age?: Age at time of response(0-18, 19-26, 27-35, 36-45, 46-60, 61+)
    Birth Sex: (Male/Female)
    How many years have you climbed?: Years of experience(0-1 year, 2-3 years, 4-5 years, 6-8 years, 9-15 years)
    What grade do you consider you climb (lead): (5.9 or less	5.10a-5.11a, 5.11b-5.11d, 5.12a-5.12d, 5.13a-5.13d, 5.14+)
    What grade do you consider you boulder?: (V0-V3, V4-V5, V6-V7, V8-V9, V10-V12, V13+)
    Have you had any climbing injuries? (check all that apply): Locations of prior/current injuries (Hand & Elbow	Shoulder, Spine, Pelvis/Hip, Knee, Foot & Ankle, Other)
    Please list your injury.: Description of specific injury (Area and what type)
    How long did the whole recovery process take before you were climbing at your same grade or better?: (Less than 1 month, 1-2 months, 2-6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, I've never recovered and climb worse, I'm still recovering)
    Did you require surgery? And are you satisfied with the surgery?: (Yes and satisfied with surgery, Yes and not satisfied with surgery, No, No, but I had prescribed physical/occupational therapy, Other (please specify))
    If so, what surgery: Description of surgery
  Potential Issues: Cannot assess injury risk due to all respondents having injuries. It also may have a bias towards a specific geographic location (Ex. Northeast US) rather than an overall survey of all climbers. 
    There are also multiple injuries per person meaning that each injury cannot be treated as independent. Due to this, it would probably not be good in estimating training load and only help in which injuries are the most common for their experience level.
    Because of that, I intent on conducting my own survey in my area and online assessing similar things but with a focus on  finger and strength training (what kind, frequency, and how difficult), and including non-injured climbers.
