Which characteristics of a rock climber's training, experience, climbing level, and climbing behavior are associated with increased risk of injury?
Injuries are a very prevalent part of climbing. As a climber myself, overtraining and overusing your muscles and tendons are easy to do and can set you back tons. 
By finding out these patterns in injuries, we can deduce how hard a climber should climb and train their muscles/tendons, and how frequently to prevent injury. 
Alongside this, finding out what specific injuries are reported would give a stance on what techniques are the most dangerous for beginners and the like to perform
and prevent overtraining of that.
In terms of data, finding out qualitative data about injuries, experience, and training alongside quantitative data of their training and climbing frequency is important.
